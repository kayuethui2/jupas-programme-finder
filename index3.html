Official Curriculum

More About CityU EE

📘 Programme Overview

  • 4-year full-time BEng programme
  • Accredited by the Hong Kong Institution of Engineers (HKIE)
  • Offered by the Department of Electrical Engineering, CityU
  • Strong focus on electronics, communications, power systems, and AI

🎓 Admission Requirements

  • JUPAS: Best 5 subjects, including Maths and one science subject
  • English Language: Level 3 or above
  • Mathematics: Level 3 or above
  • Physics or Combined Science preferred

📚 Programme Structure

  • Core Courses: 69 credit units
  • Elective Courses: at least 15 credit units
  • Final Year Project (EE4080): 6 credit units
  • Internship options: EE4085 / EE4086 / EE4087

💼 Career Prospects

  • Electronics / Hardware Engineer
  • Telecommunications Engineer
  • Power & Energy Systems Engineer
  • Semiconductor / IC Design Engineer
  • AI & Signal Processing Engineer

🏢 Internship Partners

  • Arrow Electronics
  • GP Electronics (HK) Limited
  • Sengital Limited
  • WIZnet HK Limited
  • Automated Systems (H.K.) Ltd

⭐ Why Choose CityU EE?

  • HKIE-accredited programme with strong industry links
  • Wide range of electives from AI to power systems
  • Flexible internship options (4–12 months)
  • Located in Kowloon Tong, easy access via MTR

Internship Opportunities

Real internship programmes offered by CityU Electrical Engineering

CityU EE Professional Internship Program

The Department offers structured internship courses:

  • EE4085 – Internship: Engineering Practice (4 months)
  • EE4086 – Internship: Advanced Topics
  • EE4087 – Internship: Industrial Project (up to 12 months)

These internships can replace some required courses.
